## A. Assignment Version (default on the website)

### 01 — My Typical Day

My routine changes during the week: on some days I go to the gym, while on others I attend English lessons. I like having different goals. A good routine helps me balance school, learning, and the projects I enjoy. It reminds me that progress comes from small steps.

I enjoy technology, games, design, and programming because they let me turn ideas into something useful. One of my main projects is Ada Payment, created for the Ada Mobile store. I used AI to help during development, but I designed and configured the system myself. It includes servers, products, sales, analytics, accounts, and detailed customisation. I am proud of this work. I also have PhotoForge and Asepriid, my pixel art editor.

### 03 — Things That Make Me Happy
Games and music make my free time enjoyable, and projects bring another kind of happiness. I often think of new ideas and wonder how they could become real products. I enjoy exploring possibilities, learning something new, and improving a design step by step. Creativity can start with a small thought and grow into something I can share. That feeling of discovery makes me want to keep experimenting.

## B. Extended Version (approximately 350–400 words across the three posts)

### 01 — My Typical Day
My day starts at 6:30, and school is an important part of my week. English, Computer Science, and Russian are my favourite subjects because they give me different ways to learn and think. My schedule is not exactly the same every day. On some days I go to the gym, while on others I attend English lessons. Having these different goals helps me organise my time and keep learning outside the classroom, too.

I think a useful routine is not about being busy every minute. It is about making room for responsibilities, practice, and the things you care about. Small steps can make a difference when you keep going, and different days can bring their own kind of progress.

### 02 — My Digital Life: Turning Ideas into Projects
I enjoy technology, games, design, and programming because I like exploring how ideas can become real things. One of my main projects is Ada Payment, created for the Ada Mobile store. I used AI as a development aid, but I designed and configured the system myself. The project includes servers, products, sales, analytics, accounts, and detailed customisation.

Working on a system with different parts made me think about how they fit together, not just how an individual screen looks. I am proud of Ada Payment because it represents effort and learning. I also have PhotoForge and Asepriid, my pixel art editor. I do not want to stop at one idea; I enjoy testing possibilities and discovering what I can build next.

### 03 — Things That Make Me Happy
Games and music are two things I enjoy in my free time. I also feel happy when I can spend time on a creative project or write down a new idea. Sometimes an idea begins as a simple question: what if this worked differently? From there, I can imagine different possibilities, learn something new, and improve the design step by step.

Not every idea needs to become a finished product to be worth exploring. The process can teach me something, even when I am still figuring things out. I like the feeling of curiosity that comes with making things, and I want to keep giving those ideas a chance.
